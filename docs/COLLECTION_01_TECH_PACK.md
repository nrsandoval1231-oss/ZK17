# ZK Collection 01 — Manufacturer Tech Pack v0.1
**Date:** 2026-10-25 · **Status:** specification draft / SAMPLE ONLY · **Scope:** 4 tees, 2 hoodies, 1 sweatpant

## Binding creative rules
- Use the exact ZK sword monogram from `brand/zk17-monogram-master.jpg`. See `docs/BRAND.md`. The approved monogram is the Z and K with sword as the K's spine.
- **No Japanese writing, anime references, manga characters, pagodas, moons, mountains, Japanese cultural motifs, or franchise imagery.** Earlier mockups showing these are superseded.
- Approved aesthetic: minimalist premium athletic streetwear; black, bone, washed charcoal; small controlled lavender accents; logo, clean diagonal energy strokes, negative space.
- This is a **sample brief, not an approved final production artwork file**. All dimensions below are initial targets; verify on actual blanks and approve physical strike-offs. No production run without owner signoff.

## Art and color standards
| ID | Color | HEX (digital reference only) | Print process |
|---|---|---|---|
| INK | near black | #09090D | PMS / lab match from sample |
| BONE | warm white | #F5F3F8 | opaque ink |
| CHAR | charcoal | #34343B | garment dye reference |
| LAV | muted lavender | #AC7CFF | spot ink / thread |
No print gradients, simulated embroidery, or more than 2 spot colors per placement. Require physical color approval.

**Artwork package required before vendor production:** editable outlined vector logo (SVG/PDF/EPS), single-color positive/negative variants, transparent 300dpi preview at actual dimensions, separated print plates, production placement diagrams, fonts outlined or licensed, and source files. The repo's transparent SVG is raster-mask based and is **not** equivalent to an outlined vector. Verify geometry against immutable master at 100%, 25%, and stitch/print size; remove raster background and ensure negative spaces survive. No automatic trace accepted without manual cleanup and review.

**Placement measurement convention:** all measurements on laid-flat finished garments; wearer left/right; reference chest placement measured from high-point shoulder (HPS) and center-front (CF). Tolerance targets ±10 mm screen print, ±6 mm embroidery after sampling. Garment tolerances to be agreed with vendor.

## Size and fit approval
- Ask supplier for finished-garment POM chart in cm for XS–XXL: tee chest width/body length/shoulder/sleeve; hoodie chest/body/shoulder/sleeve/hood; sweatpant waist relaxed/stretched, hip, rise, inseam, leg opening.
- Initial fit target: oversized relaxed tops, sweatpant relaxed; **do not invent numeric grading** until blanks chosen. Approve fit on two body sizes and wash after 3 cycles; record measured shrinkage.
- Sample one medium and one large tee; at least one hoodie and pant size before expanding sizes.

## Decoration / QC acceptance gates
1. Logo: compare to master with overlay and visual review; no altered Z/K geometry; sword details legible.
2. Position: within agreed placement tolerances, straight, not crossing garment seams.
3. Ink: opacity and adhesion after 3 cold wash/air-dry cycles; no unacceptable cracking, color bleed, or harsh transfer feel.
4. Garment: measurements pre/post wash, pilling, shrinkage, seam stability, no loose threads.
5. Embroidery if selected: sew-out on actual fabric; legible sword hilt; no puckering or collapsed negative space.
6. Approval: dated photo front/back, close-ups, measurement sheet, vendor quote, final art approval before bulk order.
