# Pocket Pass arcade game

Pocket Pass is a simulated arcade passing challenge launched from Gaming in the off-field cabinet. The live athlete title comes from the verified profile name; arcade markers and scores are separate from the athlete record.

Tap/click the field or adjust the aim sliders, then throw. Arrow keys aim while the field is focused and Space throws. Lead the geometric X receiver markers and avoid O defenders. The gold 17 is the quarterback marker. Four downs are available to gain ten yards; a touchdown is worth six arcade points. A round ends after twelve snaps. The pocket clock resolves an unthrown snap as a sack. Pause, restart, and exit are available throughout.

Motion uses deterministic starting routes and position-based collisions. The game starts only after Start Drive and pauses when the document becomes hidden. Scores are simulated game results and do not represent athlete performance data.
