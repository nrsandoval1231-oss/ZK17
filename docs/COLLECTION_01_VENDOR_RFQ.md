## Quote request template

Subject: Zephyr Kreye Collection 01 — seven-style decorated apparel sample RFQ

Hello,

Please quote sampling and possible small-batch production for a premium athletic/streetwear capsule: four heavyweight tees, two heavyweight hoodies and one matching sweatpant. We will supply final vector artwork after approval; please use the attached specification document for initial estimates.

Please quote:
1. Blank brand/model, material composition, GSM, available colors and size chart for each style; substitutions clearly identified.
2. Sample costs for one decorated unit per style, plus optional extra fit sample in medium and large.
3. Print method, maximum print dimensions, number of colors/locations, setup fees, and ink hand-feel; provide screen print and sample DTF options separately.
4. Any embroidery digitization / stitch-out costs as an alternate for small chest/thigh placements.
5. Minimum order quantity by style/color, price breaks at 24/50/100 units, packaging, lead time, shipping and tax.
6. Art file requirements, color matching procedure, spoilage/remake policy, pre-production proof process, and ownership of print separations/digitized files.
7. Sample shipping address and payment terms to be confirmed before invoicing.

No franchise/anime character art or Japanese motifs. Do not begin production until approved artwork, measurements, pricing and proof are signed off.

Thank you.
