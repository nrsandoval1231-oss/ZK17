# Zephyr Kreye — Apparel Drop 01 (concept, 2026-10-25)

## Identity source of truth

Use **the exact owner-approved ZK sword monogram** at `brand/zk17-monogram-master.jpg`, provenance and generated derivative pipeline at `docs/BRAND.md`. This is the existing canonical mark: angular Z and K with the sword as the K's central spine. The name **ZEPHYR KREYE** may appear as separate editorial typography, but do not bolt a new “ZK17” logo or decorative shield onto the mark. No jersey number is needed in the apparel identity.

The attached chat concept previews are **exploratory visual mockups**; they are not licensed production proofs and may simplify the monogram. Production art must reference the exact canonical source and pass owner approval. Do not create or imply licensed Berserk, anime, or other franchise collaboration: dark-fantasy sword inspiration belongs in the original abstract design, with no copyrighted characters, franchise insignia, or names printed on merchandise.

## Design thesis

Wearable beyond football. Clean, iconic, premium athletic/streetwear rather than crowded fan merchandise. Hierarchy: silhouette and fabric quality → unmistakable ZK sword mark → almost invisible supporting typography. No gradients, distressed sword illustrations, slogans everywhere, or stock anime art.

## T-shirts: design concepts

1. **Nightfall Essential** — black heavyweight 220–260 GSM boxy tee; 65–80 mm canonical monogram at wearer’s left chest in bone. Small woven hem label only. No large back print.
2. **Bone Statement** — natural off-white tee; original canonical mark centered across back at 240–300 mm tall; on the front, small type-only `ZEPHYR KREYE` lockup or tiny logo at left chest. Limit ink to black.
3. **Smoke Limited** — washed charcoal heavyweight tee; small muted-lavender left-chest monogram; blank back; minimal tonal woven neck label.

## Hats: design concepts

1. **Nightfall Crown** — structured six-panel black cap, curved brim; front-center white/bone embroidered monogram. Aim for 45–55 mm width (final dimensions subject to stitch test).
2. **Bone Crown** — six-panel natural off-white cap, black embroidered monogram; matching fabric brim.
3. **Stealth Crown** — washed charcoal cap, black-on-charcoal raised or flat embroidered monogram, subject to legibility sample.

Avoid embroidering hairline gaps too narrowly. Preserve K and sword recognition in a one-color small-size stitch-out; if needed create an independently reviewed *embroidery simplification* variant, without changing master geometry.

## Production readiness

- The master is a **JPEG raster image on black**, not a vendor-ready vector. Trace/rebuild a scalable SVG/AI/EPS asset precisely against the master **before** screen printing or digitizing embroidery; do not claim the current favicon SVG is production vector art (it embeds a raster derivative).
- Deliver separate solid-color marks for dark/light garments, with transparent background and explicit minimum size/clear space, only after visual comparison to master.
- Screen-print tees; embroidery front hat mark. Match one-color separations and test at physical size. Approve strike-offs and embroidery stitch-outs before quantity orders.
- Use blanks, dyes, and samples before pricing or purchase decisions. No supplier, licensing, or unit economics confirmed.
- Start with two lead tees (Nightfall Essential, Bone Statement) and two caps (Nightfall Crown, Bone Crown); treat Smoke and Stealth as second-wave concepts.
- No ecommerce/cart functionality is approved in this document. Website integration only after owner signs off on the apparel direction and physical samples.
