## Important findings

1. **Mobile reveal resolves after its subject has left the viewport.** `components/use-arrival-hero-motion.ts:19` uses `end: 'bottom top'` without a pin, while the athlete only starts revealing at timeline time 1 and the statement at 1.6 (`:25–27`). Measured on a 390×844 touch context: at scrollY 300 the athlete is still opacity .35 with its layer top −134.63; at scrollY 600 the athlete is finally opacity 1 but its bottom is −0.73, and REMEMBER is only opacity .8325 at y35.83. At scrollY 900 the final pullback approaches .98 with the entire athlete above the viewport (bottom −305.16). A visitor never sees the mobile final composition at full visibility. Use an independently timed mobile reveal that resolves while face/#17 and headline remain in view, or retain the complete static mobile frame with only early subtle motion. Evidence: `/tmp/hero-mobile-scroll-300.png`, `/tmp/hero-mobile-scroll-600.png`; raw measurements from `/tmp/hero-task-motion.cjs`.

2. **Touch tablets receive desktop pinning.** `components/use-arrival-hero-motion.ts:13` defines desktop solely by width≥900 and height≥650. A 1024×768 context with `isMobile:true, hasTouch:true` produced a hero pin spacer and fixed y0 from scrollY300 through 900. The approved design requires natural scrolling on touch. Require suitable input capability as well as dimensions, and check landscape tablet behavior.

3. **Photo masks leave hard rectangular backgrounds and hide the recognizable monogram.** `components/arrival-hero.module.css:4–9` fades the bottom/side edges but retains opaque top edges and large opaque backgrounds. In the 1536×1024 final render the central crop begins as a conspicuous black rectangle around y110, rear/top-right rectangles are visible, and central/right backgrounds cover nearly all the sword and Z so only separated silver wedges remain. Mobile has the same opaque black slab and disconnected mark fragments. The correct canonical PNG is used, but the required recognizable metallic ZK backdrop and clean integrated compositing are not achieved. Improve source-preserving masks/background blending and placement/mark visibility; do not fabricate missing athlete pixels. Evidence: `/tmp/hero-desktop-motion-end.png`, `/tmp/hero-desktop-static.png`, `/tmp/hero-mobile-static.png`. The absence of a full-body source is explicitly accepted and is not itself a finding.

4. **Static/no-JS fallback does not fit the complete composition into the intended viewport.** `components/arrival-hero.module.css:1` adds a full `100svh` hero after the preserved arrival wrapper's 96px top padding; mobile rules at `:11–12` force at least 780px. At direct #arrival in 1536×1024 no-JS and reduced-motion contexts, hero top=72, bottom=1096, and the editorial footer/preview label are below the viewport. At 390×844 reduced motion hero bottom=908 and footer is also below the viewport. At 320×568, headline begins at y543.58 and its second line is wholly below the viewport. The static content is present, but the promised immediate complete final composition is not. Account for header clearance and short viewports with responsive vertical sizing/spacing; retain visible face/#17.

5. **The implemented start is an already visible collage, rather than the approved darkness/rear-silhouette beat.** `components/use-arrival-hero-motion.ts:23–26` leaves rear at .15 while front/secondary portraits start at .35, the statement starts at .55, and identity/school/watch/footer remain fully visible. Initial desktop measurement confirmed these values; initial mobile also showed REMEMBER at opacity 1 while MAKE THEM was .55, because a staggered fromTo does not initialize all children together here. The first beat emphasizes the front collage/headline instead of the rear #17 silhouette, and the two-line reveal has inconsistent initial state. Preserve meaningful SSR, but explicitly initialize the enhanced beat states and choreograph the approved sequence. Evidence: `/tmp/hero-desktop-motion.png` and initial measurements in `/tmp/hero-task-motion.cjs`.

6. **Task 1 regressions do not test its highest-risk acceptance requirements.** `tests/e2e/arrival-campaign.spec.ts:20–25` tests document overflow and the absence of a spacer after toggling reduction immediately at page load. It never establishes an active pin, checks residual transforms/opacities, measures final composition while visible, or checks face/#17 crop bounds. `:10` accepts any mark bounding box even if its visible pixels are covered. There is no no-JS case, explicit 320px/short/touch-tablet case, or disabled-preview assertion in this new suite. Add targeted regressions for the repaired motion/viewport failures and meaningful final-state checks; DOM visibility alone did not detect the observed problems.
